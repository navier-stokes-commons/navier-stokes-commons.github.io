# 0.4.0-beta.6: collaboration beta with systemic assurance

This release contains 18 seed research missions, 40 bounded quests, a six-rung task ladder, 17 Initial Independent Review Portfolio quests, 5 tracked claim records, and 16 locale surfaces.

## Structural guarantees

- Release-critical facts have one writable authority. Derived projections are reconciled and checked.
- Locale and claim elevation states are evidence-derived from exact-content review records.
- Mission-to-quest, claim-to-quest, and sprint membership reverse indexes are generated rather than manually duplicated.
- Public browser rendering is exercised in CI, including JavaScript and no-JavaScript paths, narrow layouts, RTL, reduced motion, forced colors, and zoom.
- Scientific interaction is limited to declared models with static fallbacks and independent mathematical oracles.
- Collaboration transactions are machine-readable and forge-mediated rather than coupled to one host.
- Comparative visual claims are evaluated against a pre-registered reference corpus rather than asserted by a synthetic aesthetic score.

## Open-beta semantics

Open participation does not wait for independent certification of every scientific or institutional claim. Stronger claim states remain gated by the evidence class that can justify them. Negative results, reproductions, corrections, accessibility findings, design findings, and falsifications are first-class contributions when they satisfy their quest contract.
