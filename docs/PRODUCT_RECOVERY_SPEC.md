# Product recovery specification

The beta.8 R17 implementation is a baseline under indictment, not an architecture to preserve. The executor may replace it substantially if direct evidence shows the architecture is the cause.

## Non-negotiable user-facing outcome

- The site must visibly feel alive without pointer input within two seconds on English rich pages.
- Motion must read as a calm coherent fluid atmosphere across the page, not sparse hairlines or a hidden canvas.
- Horizontal and vertical pointer motion must create clearly different, broad, smooth deformation.
- The hero must read as a dense coherent fluid field and remain scientifically labeled as schematic/illustrative when it is not a quantitative simulation.
- Pause freezes all visual clocks; Resume restarts evolution without stale pointer impulse. Reduced-motion, no-JS and renderer-failure states remain deliberately composed.
- No legacy `Play sweep`, k/h/sweep-speed debug UI, or implementation-console DNA in the public flagship experience.
- Language selection must have deliberate complete composition; no accidental clipped perimeter.
- Body/UI typography must be optically legible. Avoid decorative microtype as functional UI. Verify 100%, 125%, 150%, mobile DPR and fallback-font behavior.
- Content layers must reveal rather than occlude the intended background while preserving contrast/readability.
- Mobile, keyboard, zoom/reflow and reduced-motion modes must remain operable and polished.
- `/en/math/` and other intentionally plain surfaces retain their declared product boundary.

## Architecture rule

Before changing motion code, instrument runtime ownership: loaded scripts, RAF loops, canvas creation/replacement, clear/draw writers, pointer listeners, pause/reduced-motion state owners, z-index/compositing, console errors and font/network failures. A comment claiming one authority is not evidence.

If more than one writer owns the same moving surface/state, converge to an explicit ownership graph. Prefer one motion clock and shared interaction state with explicit render targets; do not preserve dual authorities for compatibility theater.

## Design search

Do not serially tune R17. Generate at least three materially different candidate directions before committing to a redesign: e.g. field/gradient-dominant, particle-advection-dominant, or hybrid layered field. They must differ in architecture/composition, not just opacity or particle count. Use blind comparative review and the hostile lane to eliminate reward-hacked candidates.
