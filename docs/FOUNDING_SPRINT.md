Produce the first independently reviewable evidence across proof audit, formal semantics, substantive mathematics, scientific computation, and collaboration integrity.

## Completion semantics

The sprint is public work, not a pre-launch blocker. Its success conditions are the canonical conditions in `content/public/founding_sprint.json`, evaluated from public artifacts and review records. The first accepted contribution should exercise the complete lifecycle: attempt, artifact, evidence, independent review, canonical record, deterministic rebuild, and public projection.
