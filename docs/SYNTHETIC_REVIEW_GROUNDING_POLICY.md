# Synthetic-review grounding policy

A role title is not a reviewer. A résumé string is not a method. A persona is not evidence.

## Expert evaluators

Expert contracts specify the minimum competence needed, but release authority comes from the **observational procedure**, not the claimed biography. Each run must show what artifact was inspected, by what modality, under what blindness conditions, with what prompt/model version, and which direct observations support the verdict.

## Synthetic user strata

User simulations are organized around task, prior knowledge, device constraints, accessibility stress, language/script and motivation rather than demographic stereotypes. They may establish what the particular synthetic evaluator perceived in the supplied evidence. They may not estimate prevalence among real people or substitute for lived-experience research.

When real interview/usability data exists, synthetic strata should be grounded in those transcripts/observations instead of invented biographies. The underlying source must be consent-compatible and its provenance recorded. Until then, task-conditioned strata are deliberately narrow.

## Reviewer authority

Synthetic stakeholder evidence has asymmetric authority:

- A valid, evidence-grounded P0/P1 finding can veto a release candidate.
- Missing evidence can force ABSTAIN and block a required criterion.
- A synthetic PASS can close only the specified defect-discovery criterion; it cannot certify broad beauty, motivation, cultural fit, accessibility lived experience, or population preference.

This asymmetry is intentional. Defect discovery often needs only one concrete counterexample. Population inference needs representative external data.

## Independence

Blind first-impression lanes see only the rendered artifact and assigned task. Specialist lanes do not see peer verdicts before their own observation. The hostile lane is intentionally allowed to see the existing gates so it can reward-hack them. Model/provider identity and independence group are recorded on every run.
