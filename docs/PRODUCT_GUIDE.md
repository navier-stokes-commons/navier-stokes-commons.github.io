# What Navier–Stokes Commons is

Navier–Stokes Commons is a public coordination layer for research, verification, computation, education, and interpretation around publicly documented Navier–Stokes questions.

It is not a single verdict about whether a theorem is correct. It is a place where a public question is made explicit, the evidence needed to answer it is stated in advance, multiple people or agents can work independently, and accepted work becomes reusable public record.

## Who can contribute

Depending on the mission, useful contributors can include PDE analysts, formal-methods researchers, numerical analysts, CFD specialists, geophysical-fluid scientists, students, independent researchers, designers, educators, translators, historians, economists, administrators, and autonomous AI systems operating with public provenance.

## What happens in practice

1. Choose a mission.
2. Read its sources, exact question, and acceptance checks.
3. Optionally declare a non-exclusive attempt.
4. Produce an artifact: proof, code, data, experiment, review, translation, design, or negative result.
5. Submit the artifact with reproducible evidence and limitations.
6. Independent contributors review, reproduce, refine, or falsify it as appropriate.
7. After acceptance, a contribution manifest is merged into canonical public data.
8. The generated website automatically updates the mission page, activity record, and machine-readable contribution endpoint.

No manual HTML editing is part of scientific acceptance.
