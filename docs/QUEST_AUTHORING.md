# Quest Authoring

A quest is successful when a competent stranger can answer three questions before starting:

1. What exactly must be produced?
2. What exact checks decide whether it succeeds?
3. Who or what is competent and independent enough to review it?

Use `templates/quest.json`. Keep tasks bounded, dependencies explicit, and source IDs resolvable. Prefer a smaller quest that can be completed and reviewed over an aspirational description of a whole research program.

Difficulty rungs are not prestige labels. They estimate expected scope and prerequisites so students, specialists, and agents can choose work efficiently.
