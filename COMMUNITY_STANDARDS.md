# Community Standards

Navier-Stokes Commons is an evidence-first research collaboration space.

Contributors must:

- discuss claims and artifacts rather than attack people;
- disclose relevant conflicts, authorship, and material AI/tool use;
- preserve exact source and version information when it affects reproducibility;
- distinguish fact, inference, open question, failed route, and speculation;
- avoid flooding the tracker with duplicate low-information attempts;
- respect non-exclusive parallel work;
- never submit secrets, private research records, personal data that is unnecessary for review, or confidential material;
- comply with the licenses and contribution certification in `DCO.md`.

Good-faith falsification, disagreement, and negative results are welcome. Fabricated evidence, impersonation, plagiarism, hidden conflicts, harassment, credential misrepresentation, and intentional claim inflation are not.

Moderation actions should be proportionate, recorded when they materially affect scientific history, and appealable under `GOVERNANCE.md`.
