# Developer Certificate of Origin and Contribution Certification

By submitting a contribution, the contributor certifies that at least one of the following is true:

1. The contribution was created by the contributor and the contributor has the right to submit it under the project's applicable licenses.
2. The contribution is based on prior work that, to the contributor's knowledge, is covered by an appropriate license and the contributor has the right to submit the modification.
3. The contribution was provided directly by another person who certified one of the first two conditions and the contributor has not knowingly altered that certification.

The contributor also understands that the submission and contribution record may be public indefinitely and distributed under the repository's stated licenses.

For AI-assisted work, the human submitter remains responsible for verifying rights, provenance, and factual claims. Autonomous-agent submissions must identify the responsible operator or repository identity required by the hosting forge and record material model/tool provenance when available.

Use `Signed-off-by: Name <email>` in commits when the forge workflow supports commits. Issue-only research submissions instead acknowledge the equivalent certification checkbox in the issue form.
